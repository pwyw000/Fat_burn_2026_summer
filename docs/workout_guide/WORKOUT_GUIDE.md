# Strength workout guide (20–30 min)

Equipment: dumbbells, a kettlebell, an adjustable bench. No cables or bands.

Each "Watch" link opens a YouTube search for the exercise, so you can pick a demo from any coach you like.

**Every session:**

- Warm up 2 min, including 1 light set of the first move.
- 3 rounds per pair: do both moves back to back, then rest 60–75 s.
- Stop each set with about 2 reps left.
- Whoop yellow or red: 2 rounds instead of 3.
- Stop at 30 min.

## Why these exercises

- **Rear delts:** In ACE's EMG study, the seated rear lateral raise scored 73% MVC and the 45° incline row 69%, the top two. The row alone is enough, so no reverse fly. [ACE](https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/prosource/research-special-issue-2015/5320/dynamite-delts-ace-research-identifies-top-shoulder-exercises/)
- **Back:** Chest-supported rows were Jeff Nippard's top-ranked back exercise. The pullover loads the lats in a stretched position. [BarBend](https://barbend.com/news/jeff-nippard-ranks-best-and-worst-back-exercises/)
- **Chest:** Upper-chest activation peaked with the bench at 30°, and the middle and lower chest peaked at 0°. Above 45°, the work shifts to the front delts. [PMC7579505](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7579505/) Presses activated the chest more than flies, so the fly is the accessory. [PMC7675616](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7675616/)
- **Abs:** Bicycle crunches scored 248% of a standard crunch, the highest of the exercises tested. Reverse crunches ranked 3rd for obliques. Planks and plain crunches scored low. [ACE PDF](https://www.acefitness.org/getfit/studies/BestWorstAbExercises.pdf)
- **Biceps:** Incline curls grew the biceps more than preacher curls on MRI (+18.0% vs +14.9%). Hammer curls cover the brachialis and brachioradialis. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39809454/)
- **Triceps:** Overhead extensions produced about 1.4× more whole-triceps growth than neutral-arm extensions (+19.9% vs +13.9%). [Maeo 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/17461391.2022.2100279)
- **Side delts:** Dumbbell and cable lateral raises grew the side delts equally, so no cable is needed. [Frontiers 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12277279/)
- **Legs:** Bulgarian split squats train quads, glutes and hamstrings. Leaning forward shifts work to the glutes and hamstrings. [PMC12382192](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12382192/) Standing (straight-knee) calf raises grew the calves more than seated ones.
- **Once a week per muscle is fine:** With weekly sets equal, training a muscle 1× vs 2× per week showed no meaningful difference in growth. [Schoenfeld 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30558493)
- **Stretched positions:** Training at long muscle lengths has a small growth edge (ES ≈ 0.28), so the plan favors the incline curl, overhead extension, pullover and paused fly. [2025 meta](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11332-025-01586-5)
